## 1. Introduction
This document provides comprehensive training materials for compliance awareness and regulatory requirements within the Zenith Fraud Detection Platform. All personnel are required to complete relevant training modules annually to maintain compliance certification.

## 2. Training Overview

### 2.1 Training Objectives
- Understand regulatory requirements and compliance obligations
- Recognize compliance risks and reporting requirements
- Apply compliance principles in daily operations
- Maintain awareness of emerging regulatory changes

### 2.2 Training Requirements
- **All Personnel**: Basic compliance awareness (annual)
- **Compliance Team**: Advanced regulatory training (quarterly)
- **Technical Staff**: Security and privacy training (biannual)
- **Management**: Leadership compliance training (annual)

## 3. Core Training Modules

### 3.1 AML Fundamentals (Required)
**Duration**: 45 minutes
**Target Audience**: All personnel
**Learning Objectives**:
- Understand money laundering concepts and techniques
- Recognize suspicious activities and red flags
- Know reporting requirements and procedures
- Apply customer due diligence principles

**Key Topics**:
- Money laundering stages (placement, layering, integration)
- Common ML techniques and indicators
- Regulatory reporting obligations (SAR/CTR filing)
- Customer identification and verification procedures

### 3.2 Fraud Detection Techniques (Required)
**Duration**: 60 minutes
**Target Audience**: Analysts and investigators
**Learning Objectives**:
- Apply advanced fraud detection methodologies
- Use analytical tools and techniques effectively
- Interpret fraud patterns and indicators
- Document findings for legal proceedings

**Key Topics**:
- Behavioral analysis and anomaly detection
- Transaction pattern recognition
- Evidence collection and chain of custody
- Case documentation and reporting

### 3.3 Regulatory Compliance (Required)
**Duration**: 90 minutes
**Target Audience**: Compliance team and management
**Learning Objectives**:
- Understand FATF, GDPR, and SOX requirements
- Implement compliance monitoring and controls
- Conduct compliance assessments and audits
- Manage regulatory relationships and reporting

**Key Topics**:
- FATF 40 Recommendations implementation
- GDPR data protection principles
- SOX financial reporting controls
- Regulatory examination preparation

### 3.4 Security Incident Response (Required)
**Duration**: 75 minutes
**Target Audience**: Technical staff and security team
**Learning Objectives**:
- Respond effectively to security incidents
- Contain and mitigate security threats
- Document incidents and lessons learned
- Coordinate with regulatory authorities

**Key Topics**:
- Incident classification and prioritization
- Containment and eradication procedures
- Evidence preservation and forensic analysis
- Communication and reporting protocols

### 3.5 Data Privacy and Protection (Optional)
**Duration**: 60 minutes
**Target Audience**: All personnel handling personal data
**Learning Objectives**:
- Understand data privacy principles and obligations
- Apply data protection controls and procedures
- Recognize data breach indicators and responses
- Maintain compliance with privacy regulations

**Key Topics**:
- GDPR and CCPA compliance requirements
- Personal data handling procedures
- Privacy impact assessments
- Data breach notification procedures

### 3.6 Cyber Security Awareness (Optional)
**Duration**: 30 minutes
**Target Audience**: All personnel
**Learning Objectives**:
- Recognize common cyber security threats
- Apply safe computing practices
- Report suspicious activities and incidents
- Protect sensitive information

**Key Topics**:
- Phishing and social engineering attacks
- Password security and multi-factor authentication
- Safe internet and email practices
- Incident reporting procedures
