To ensure 10/10 technical findings are admissible in a court of law, all reports must follow these forensic standards.

Every report generated by the platform must include:
*   **Admissibility Assertion**: A verified statement that the evidence was processed via the **Immutable Audit Chain**.
*   **Theory of Intent (Mens Rea)**: Clear classification of the subject's state of mind:
    *   **Level 1: Knowledge** (Subject was aware of the anomaly).
    *   **Level 2: Intent** (Subject actively bypassed controls).
    *   **Level 3: Willful Blindness** (Subject ignored systemic alerts).
*   **Chain of Custody Summary**: A certified log of who touched the data and when.

Reports must be structured for immediate handoff to legal counsel:

4.  **Mens Rea Indicators**: Bulleted evidence proving knowledge/motive.

## 3. Visualization Requirements
*   **The "Jury-Ready" Graph**: Entity relationship maps must use high-contrast, labeled nodes.
